INSTALL

Installing a theme

You need the SD card RiftWii runs from and a computer that can open zip files.

Download

Pick a theme above and download its zip.

Copy to the SD card

Unzip it into sd:/riftwii/themes/. The zip already holds the theme's folder, so you end up with sd:/riftwii/themes/<name>/theme.ini.

Choose it

On the Wii, open RiftWii, go to Settings > Menu > Theme and step to the new theme. RiftWii offers to restart its menu to show it.

MAKE YOUR OWN

Make your own

A theme is a small folder: a theme.ini with its colors and shape, plus any pictures, music or sounds you want to replace. RiftWii's theme guide lists every picture and its exact size, and its theme kit can paint the pictures in your colors.

Build and try it

Put your folder in sd:/riftwii/themes/ and test it on your Wii, or in Dolphin.

Add a LICENSE

Choose CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0 or CC0, and credit anything you used from someone else.

Open a pull request

Add your folder under themes/ in RiftWii/themes. The automatic check tells you straight away if anything needs fixing.

Only artwork, music and fonts you made yourself, or have permission to share.
